## invoice_origin rules — read carefully, this is the most important field

**UNPAID documents** (money has NOT yet been paid — use these when the document is a request for payment):
- `pt_at_invoice` — Portuguese "Fatura" (FT) or "Fatura Simplificada" (FS) issued under Portuguese tax law (AT/ATCUD present). Signals: "fatura", "ATCUD", Portuguese NIF, Multibanco reference "para pagamento", "prazo de pagamento", "data limite", "valor a pagar".
- `international` — Foreign invoice not issued under Portuguese AT rules. Signals: non-PT seller, no ATCUD, amounts in non-EUR or mixed language.

**PAID documents** (money HAS already been received — use these when the document confirms a completed payment):
- `fatura_recibo` — Portuguese "Fatura-Recibo" (FR): a combined invoice AND receipt issued at point of sale — payment was made immediately. Signals: "fatura-recibo", "fatura recibo", document type FR, "recebido", "liquidado".
- `receipt` — A receipt or "Recibo" (RC) for a payment already completed. Signals: "recibo", "receipt", "comprovativo", "pago", "paid", store receipt, online shop order confirmed.
- `payment_confirmation` — Confirmation that a card, MBWay, PayPal, or similar digital payment went through. Signals: "pagamento confirmado", "pagamento efetuado", "payment received", "payment successful", MBWay confirmation.
- `bank_transfer` — Confirmation of a completed bank transfer. Signals: "transferência efetuada", "wire transfer sent", "bank transfer confirmed".

## Key distinctions (common mistakes to avoid)

- A "Fatura" with a Multibanco reference is UNPAID — it is a payment request, not a receipt. Use `pt_at_invoice`.
- A "Fatura-Recibo" means both invoice AND receipt in one — it IS paid. Use `fatura_recibo`.
- "Recibo de pagamento" or "comprovativo de pagamento" = already paid. Use `receipt` or `payment_confirmation`.
- "Para pagamento", "prazo de pagamento", "referência para pagamento" = NOT yet paid.
- If the email contains only a Multibanco entity+reference+amount but no confirmation message, the invoice is UNPAID.
